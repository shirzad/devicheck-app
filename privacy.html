UltraCheck Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy covers the UltraCheck app for iPhone.

The short version

  • The app has no account, no login, no advertising and no analytics.
  • When you use the app on its own, your test results stay on your device.
  • Results leave the device only if the device is connected to an UltraCheck workstation, which repair and refurbishment businesses use.

Using the app on its own

The tests run on your device. The app does not send your results, or anything else about you, to us or to anyone else.

One test makes a network request: the Wi-Fi test loads a small public test page from Apple (www.apple.com) to check that the internet connection works. The request carries no information from the app. As with any web request, Apple's server sees your IP address.

What is stored on your device

The app keeps the progress and results of the current test run in its own storage on the device, so a run can continue after an interruption. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Using the app with an UltraCheck workstation

Repair and refurbishment businesses can connect a device by USB to UltraCheck workstation software on their computer. In that case the app sends the following to that workstation, over USB or the local Wi-Fi network:

  • the result of each test (for example Pass or Fail), with its technical measurements and timing
  • a random identifier for the test run
  • the app version and the iOS version

The app does not send your name, contacts, photos, audio recordings, location coordinates or any account information, because it has none of these.

The workstation software can read device details such as the model and serial number directly over the USB connection, as any computer can once the device trusts it. The business that operates the workstation decides how long reports are kept and is your contact for them.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell data, do not share it with advertisers, and do not use it to track you across apps or websites. The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.

Children

The app is a hardware testing tool. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you have the right to ask for access to your personal data, and for its correction or deletion, and you can complain to your data protection authority. To use these rights, write to the email address above.
