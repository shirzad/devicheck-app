UltraCheck Support

UltraCheck is an iPhone and iPad app that tests the hardware of the device it runs on: touch screen, cameras, speakers, microphones, buttons, sensors, Face ID, NFC, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, location and barometer.

Contact

Questions, problems or feedback: khalilarbab80@gmail.com

Please include your iPhone or iPad model, the iOS version and the test step you were on. That makes it much faster to help.

Common questions

A test shows "Not tested"

The test needs a permission that was not granted, for example camera or microphone access. Open Settings › UltraCheck, switch the permission on and run the test again.

A test shows "N/A"

Your device does not have that part. For example, not every iPhone has a telephoto camera.

A test shows "Review" or "Incomplete"

The app could not reach a clear result. This often happens in a loud room, with a thick case, or with a screen protector that covers a sensor. Remove the case and repeat the test in a quiet place.

The Wi-Fi test does not pass

The Wi-Fi test needs a Wi-Fi network with internet access. Connect to one and repeat the test.

Does the app send my data anywhere?

Used on its own, no. See the privacy policy for the details.
